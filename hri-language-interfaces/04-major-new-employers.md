# Major and New Employers

## Established

- **Google DeepMind Robotics / Gemini Robotics** — SayCan lineage.
- **Amazon (Astro, Alexa+, Lab126)** — voice + embodied agents.
- **Boston Dynamics** — Spot SDK + LLM integration team.
- **Toyota Research Institute** — fleet learning + HRI.
- **Diligent Robotics** — Moxi for hospitals (one of the longest-running
  HRI startups).
- **Bear Robotics, Pudu, Keenon** — service robots in restaurants and
  hotels.
- **Apple** — internal home-robotics initiatives.

## Companies founded 2020-2025 (or newly scaled)

- **Robust.AI** (2019, scaled 2023+, Rodney Brooks) — Carter collab
  cart, HRI focus.
- **Hello Robot Stretch 3** (HRI-focused mobile manipulator, scaled
  2023+).
- **Sanctuary AI** (Phoenix) — natural-language control demos.
- **Apptronik** — Apollo voice-task demos.
- **1X NEO** — household HRI focus.
- **Figure** — voice + Helix.
- **Wing (Alphabet) / Zipline** — drone delivery HRI for handoffs.
- **Chef Robotics** — kitchen robot with operator UX.
- **Daedalus** (2024) — manufacturing copilot UX.
- **Spot AI / Verkada** (security camera + LLM event description).
- **Multion / Adept / Imbue** (general AI agents adjacent to HRI).
- **Inflection / Pi (consumer voice agent)** — not robotics but feeds
  the HRI talent pool.
- **Tangram Vision, Tangible (robot UX shops)** — small teams hired by
  OEMs.

## Where HRI engineers earn the most

Generally a bit lower than core ML — $160-220k median — but HRI leads
at top humanoid startups (Figure, 1X, Apptronik) clear $250k+ TC. The
fast money is at the HRI-meets-VLA boundary.
