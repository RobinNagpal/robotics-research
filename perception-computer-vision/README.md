# Perception & Computer Vision

The workhorse layer of every robot — turning raw camera/LiDAR/depth pixels
into structured understanding (objects, poses, geometry, semantics).

Currently being upended by neural representations: NeRF, 3D Gaussian
Splatting, learned depth, and vision foundation models (SAM 2, DINOv2,
Depth-Anything).

One of the top-3 picks: highest demand, broadest customer base, easiest
demos (a laptop webcam is often enough hardware).
