# Major and New Employers

## Established giants (still hiring heavily)

- **Waymo** (Alphabet) — AV perception, $232k median, large team.
- **NVIDIA** — Isaac, GR00T, foundation perception; $270k median.
- **Mobileye** — vision-centric ADAS, Jerusalem + US.
- **Boston Dynamics / Hyundai** — Spot, Atlas perception.
- **Skydio** — autonomous drones (founded 2014, scaled past 2022).
- **Zoox** (Amazon) — full AV stack.
- **Tesla Autopilot/Optimus** — camera-only perception and humanoid.
- **Apple** — Vision Pro, computer vision for AR.
- **Meta Reality Labs** — Aria, SAM, DINOv2 teams.
- **Symbotic** — warehouse robotics perception.

## Companies founded in the last 3-5 years (2020-2025)

- **Wayve** (UK, founded 2017 but Series-C in 2024, $1B+) — end-to-end
  driving foundation models, large CV team.
- **Cobot** (2022, Brad Porter ex-Amazon Robotics VP) — collaborative
  mobile manipulator; perception-heavy stack.
- **Bedrock Robotics** (2023) — autonomous earth-moving equipment.
- **Saronic** (2022) — autonomous surface vessels (defense).
- **Anduril** (perception side ramped 2021+) — autonomy stack for
  defense; large CV team for ISR and counter-UAS.
- **Shield AI** (Hivemind, scaled 2022+) — autonomous flight, $228k median.
- **Physical Intelligence** (2024) — VLA models; uses heavy perception
  inputs.
- **Skild AI** (2023, ex-CMU) — general robot brain, perception-heavy.
- **1X Technologies** (rebranded 2022, Series-B 2024) — humanoids.
- **Figure AI** (2022) — humanoid with strong vision stack (Helix).
- **Apptronik** (Apollo humanoid, 2023 commercial push).
- **Rivr** (2023, Switzerland, ex-ETH) — last-mile delivery robots.
- **Pickle Robot** (2018, scaled 2023+) — truck-unloading perception.
- **Field AI** (2023) — outdoor field-robot foundation models.
- **Chef Robotics** (2019, scaled past 2023) — food assembly perception.

## Where perception engineers earn the most

NVIDIA, Waymo, Apple, Meta, Anthropic robotics, Physical Intelligence,
Wayve, Anduril, Shield AI, Skild — all in the $220k-$350k+ TC range for
senior IC.
