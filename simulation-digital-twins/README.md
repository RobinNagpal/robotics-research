# Simulation & Digital Twins

Building photoreal, physics-accurate simulators and digital twins for
training policies, validating fleets, and replacing expensive real-world
data collection.

The dark horse of the top 3: zero hardware required, **fastest-growing
market segment in robotics** by CAGR (~28.5%, $3.8B in 2025 -> $34.6B by
2034), and every humanoid/AMR startup is buying.

One of the top-3 picks: highest pure-software ratio of any robotics
subfield.
