# Major and New Employers

## Established

- **NVIDIA** — Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab, Omniverse, GR00T, Cosmos; the
  single largest robotics-sim employer. $270k median.
- **Google DeepMind** — MuJoCo team + sim infra for RT-X, Gemini
  Robotics.
- **Microsoft Project AirSim / Mesh / Azure Digital Twins**.
- **Apple, Meta Reality Labs** — sim for AR/embodied AI.
- **Toyota Research Institute** — DRAKE + manipulation sim.
- **Siemens, ABB, FANUC, KUKA** — industrial sim suites.
- **Unity, Unreal/Epic** — engine vendors expanding into robotics sim.
- **Ansys, Altair, Dassault Systemes** — CAE/CFD with robotics
  integration.

## Companies founded 2020-2025

- **Foretellix** (founded 2018, scaled 2023+) — scenario-based AV
  validation.
- **Applied Intuition** (2017, scaled to multi-billion 2023+) — AV
  simulation + ADAS test platforms.
- **Parallel Domain** (2017, scaled 2023+) — synthetic data for AV /
  perception.
- **Cesium ion + Cesium for Omniverse** — geospatial digital twins.
- **Genesis project** (CMU + others, 2024) — open universal sim.
- **Spear AI / Cogniteam / Foxglove** — observability + sim adjacent
  tooling for robotics teams.
- **RAI Inst. / Hello Robot / Reflex Robotics** — internal sim teams.
- **NVIDIA Cosmos partner ecosystem** — many new startups building on
  top of Cosmos.
- **Hillbot** (2024, ex-Stanford) — generative sim assets.
- **Diffuse Bio / Diffuse Drive (small but emerging)** — diffusion-based
  sim asset generation.
- **Skild AI, Physical Intelligence, Figure, 1X, Apptronik** — all
  have growing sim teams to feed their VLA training.

## Where sim/twin engineers earn the most

NVIDIA tops the list ($270k median; senior staff $400k+ TC). Applied
Intuition and Parallel Domain pay competitively. Sim/RL engineers at
top humanoid startups (Figure, 1X, Physical Intelligence) regularly
clear $300k TC.

## Market signal

Robotic Simulator market is software-dominated (~72% in 2025);
Physical-AI simulation & digital-twin market projected $3.8B (2025) ->
$34.6B (2034), **~28.5% CAGR — highest of any robotics segment**.
