# Major and New Employers

Who actually pays VLA engineers, and what to know about each. Salary
numbers below come from public sources: levels.fyi, the 2025 Robotics
Salary Guide, and reported funding rounds. Treat them as bands, not
guarantees.

## Big tech and established research labs

These are the equivalent of "FAANG for robot learning." Bigger teams,
more bureaucracy, slightly less upside, but stable comp and great
mentors.

- **Google DeepMind Robotics** — the team behind RT-1/RT-2/RT-X and
  Gemini Robotics. London, Mountain View. Publishes a lot.
- **NVIDIA GEAR / Isaac team** — Jim Fan's group, behind GR00T.
  Reports of ~$270k median TC, top-of-band over $400k. NVIDIA equity
  has been a huge tailwind for the last few years.
- **Tesla Optimus** — humanoid policies. Famously secretive and
  high-pressure.
- **Meta AI / FAIR Robotics** — Habitat simulator, Embodied AI
  research. Less product, more papers.
- **Apple** — robotics rumored, hiring quiet. Top-of-market comp.
- **Amazon Robotics / Lab126** — manipulation, Astro home robot, the
  warehouse stack.
- **Toyota Research Institute (TRI)** — pioneered Diffusion Policy.
  Cambridge MA + Los Altos. A great place to do research-grade work.

## VLA-native startups founded or scaled 2020-2025

This is where the explosive growth (and compensation) is. Many of
these did not exist 3 years ago.

- **Physical Intelligence (Pi)** (founded 2024) — pi0, pi0-FAST,
  pi0.5. Raised $400M Nov 2024 at $2.4B valuation. The "OpenAI of
  robotics." Open-weights for some models, generalist focus.
- **Skild AI** (2023) — "Skild Brain," robot-agnostic generalist
  policy. $300M Series A July 2024 at ~$1.5B valuation.
- **Figure AI** (2022) — Figure 02 humanoid + Helix VLA. Cumulative
  >$1.5B raised; reported $39.5B valuation talks in Feb 2025.
- **1X Technologies** (rebranded from Halodi 2022) — NEO consumer
  humanoid + world-model-based policies.
- **Apptronik** (Apollo humanoid, commercial push 2023+) — Mercedes
  pilots. Apollo is one of the more credible commercial humanoids.
- **Sanctuary AI** (Phoenix robot, scaled 2023+) — "Carbon"
  cognitive architecture; Canadian.
- **Generalist** (2024) — ex-Tesla / OpenAI founders, foundation
  models for humanoids. Pre-product, hiring aggressively.
- **Reflex Robotics** (2023) — mobile humanoid for warehouses.
- **Mentee Robotics** (Israel, 2022) — Menteebot humanoid.
- **Persona AI** (2024) — software-first humanoid stack.
- **K-Scale Labs** (2024) — open-source humanoid for developers.
  Think "Arduino for humanoids."
- **Field AI** (2023) — outdoor / off-road foundation policies for
  inspection, construction.
- **Daedalus** (2024) — manufacturing-focused robot brain.
- **Cobot** (2022) — collaborative mobile manipulator built on
  learned policies. Strong eng team.

## How they hire (so you can target effectively)

Most of the above hire ML engineers in three buckets:

- **Research scientist** — PhD or equivalent, publishes papers.
  Hardest to break in cold; often hired straight from labs.
- **Research engineer** — strong eng + decent ML chops. **This is
  the realistic target for a junior web dev with ~6 months of
  ramp-up.** You'll glue datasets, run training, optimize inference.
- **Infra / data engineer** — pipelines, dataloaders, eval harnesses,
  serving. Your existing web-eng skills already overlap a lot.

## Where VLA engineers earn the most

Approximate TC bands for senior IC (3-7 years experience), 2025:

- NVIDIA: $300-450k
- Physical Intelligence / Skild / Figure / 1X: $350-600k+ (heavy
  equity; the equity is the lottery ticket)
- Google DeepMind / Tesla / Apple: $300-500k
- TRI / Meta FAIR: $250-400k

These numbers assume Bay Area / NYC; remote/EU is usually 20-40% lower.

## Hiring market signal

From the 2025 Robotics Salary Guide:

- ML Engineer is the **second-highest-paying IC track** in robotics
  (after embedded real-time + Linux).
- "RL" and "diffusion" each command a **+33% salary premium** when
  listed as a required skill.
- ML appears in **~31% of all robotics job postings** — the single
  most-requested skill family in the field.

Translation: this is the easiest robotics specialty to convert a
generic ML / SWE background into a job in, and it pays the most. The
catch is that demand is so visible that the bar is rising fast — get
in now while "OpenVLA fine-tuned on 100 episodes" is still an
impressive portfolio item.
