# Major and New Employers

## Established

- **Amazon Robotics** — WES (Warehouse Execution System) is one of the
  largest multi-robot software systems in the world.
- **Symbotic** — fleet of 500-2000 bots per site.
- **Geek+ / GreyOrange / Locus Robotics / 6 River Systems / Fetch
  (Zebra)** — warehouse fleets.
- **Ocado Technology** — grid swarm.
- **Waymo, Cruise, Zoox, Aurora** — vehicle fleets and dispatch.
- **DJI Enterprise / Skydio Dock** — drone fleet dock products.
- **Brain Corp** — multi-floor-scrubber fleet software.
- **Open Robotics (Open-RMF)** — open-source ecosystem (sponsored by
  Intrinsic / Alphabet).

## Companies founded 2020-2025

- **Shield AI** (Hivemind, scaled 2022+, $228k median) — multi-drone
  teaming.
- **Anduril** — Lattice OS coordinates many heterogeneous unmanned
  platforms.
- **Saronic** (2022) — multi-USV maritime patrols.
- **Saildrone** (older but scaled past 2022) — autonomous sailing fleet.
- **Verity** (drone inventory, scaled 2022+) — multi-drone indoor.
- **Skydio Dock** (2023 commercial) — fleet of autonomous drones.
- **Bedrock Robotics** (2023) — multi-machine construction fleets.
- **Outrider** (yard trucks, scaled 2022+).
- **Gather AI** (drone inventory) — multi-drone.
- **Pegasus / FlytBase** — drone fleet management SaaS.
- **Formant, InOrbit, Freedom Robotics, Foxglove** — fleet observability
  + ops SaaS (small but rapidly growing companies, all founded
  2018-2022).
- **Intrinsic (Alphabet)** — Flowstate + Open-RMF investments.
- **Diligent Robotics** — fleet of Moxis per hospital.

## Where multi-robot engineers earn the most

Amazon Robotics, Waymo, Shield AI ($228k median), Anduril, Symbotic,
NVIDIA. Defense and AV (vehicle and drone) pay the highest premium.
Fleet-software ICs at top warehouse companies routinely $200-300k TC.
