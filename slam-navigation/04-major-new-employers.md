# Major and New Employers

## Established giants

- **Waymo, Cruise, Zoox, Pony.ai, Aurora, Motional** — full AV stacks.
- **NVIDIA Isaac / DRIVE** — perception + SLAM toolchains.
- **Boston Dynamics** — Spot Autowalk, Atlas.
- **Skydio** — drone VIO.
- **Amazon Robotics / Symbotic** — warehouse fleet localization.
- **iRobot, Roborock, Ecovacs** — consumer floor robots.
- **Locus Robotics, Geek+, Fetch (acquired by Zebra), 6 River Systems** —
  warehouse AMRs.
- **DJI** — drone VIO.

## Companies founded 2020-2025 (or scaled then)

- **Wayve** (UK) — large mapping-free driving team.
- **Wayve / Nuro / Gatik** — last-mile and middle-mile AVs.
- **Saronic** (2022) — autonomous surface vessels; maritime SLAM.
- **Bedrock Robotics** (2023) — outdoor SLAM on construction equipment.
- **Field AI** (2023) — outdoor / off-road navigation foundation models.
- **Cobot** (2022) — indoor mobile manipulator, Nav2-style stack at
  scale.
- **Pickle Robot** (2018, scaled past 2023) — truck-trailer mapping.
- **Anduril Ghost / Bolt / Roadrunner** — UAV/UGV navigation.
- **Shield AI** (Hivemind) — GPS-denied flight, fast-growing.
- **Reflex Robotics** (2023) — humanoid mobile base.
- **Diligent Robotics** (Moxi, scaled 2022+) — hospital corridors.
- **Brain Corp** (continues to scale) — floor-scrubber autonomy SDK.
- **Verity** (drones in warehouses, scaled 2022+) — indoor drone SLAM.
- **Exotec** (Skypod, France) — 3D warehouse navigation.

## Where SLAM/nav engineers earn the most

Waymo ($232k median), NVIDIA ($270k), Tesla AV, Apple, Wayve, Shield AI
($228k), Skydio, Zoox. Defense (Anduril, Shield AI, Saronic) pays
competitively and is the fastest-hiring vertical in 2024-25.
