# Motion Planning & Control

Turning a desired task into safe, smooth, dynamically feasible motion —
trajectory optimization, MPC, whole-body control, and (increasingly)
learned controllers that replace or augment classical ones.

Classical core of robotics that is being modernized by RL, diffusion
policies, and differentiable physics.
