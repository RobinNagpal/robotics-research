# Major and New Employers

Who pays simulation / digital-twin engineers, and what to know about
each. Numbers from levels.fyi, the 2025 Robotics Salary Guide, and
public funding rounds. Treat as bands, not guarantees.

## Big tech and established labs

These are the equivalent of "FAANG for sim." Bigger teams, more
process, top-of-band comp.

- **NVIDIA (Isaac, GR00T, Omniverse, Cosmos)** — the single largest
  robotics-sim employer on Earth. The teams behind Isaac Sim, Isaac
  Lab, Cosmos, Replicator, USD tooling. Reported **~$270k median TC**,
  senior staff $400k+. Multiple US offices + Tel Aviv + Munich.
- **Google DeepMind** — MuJoCo team + sim infra for RT-X, Gemini
  Robotics. Mostly London / Mountain View. Famously hard to break
  into.
- **Microsoft (Project AirSim, Mesh, Azure Digital Twins)** — sim
  for AV / robotics / industrial. Redmond + Cambridge UK. AirSim is
  archived but Microsoft continues to invest in successor work.
- **Apple, Meta Reality Labs** — sim for AR / embodied AI / Habitat.
  Strong research culture; Habitat 3.0 is from Meta.
- **Toyota Research Institute (TRI)** — Drake + manipulation sim.
  Cambridge MA + Los Altos. Excellent place for research-grade
  engineering work; comp $250-400k.
- **Siemens, ABB, FANUC, KUKA** — industrial sim suites (Process
  Simulate, RobotStudio, ROBOGUIDE). European / Japanese; lower comp
  than US tech, but stable.
- **Unity, Unreal / Epic** — engine vendors expanding into robotics
  sim. Unity Industrial Collection is the headline product on
  Unity's side.
- **Ansys, Altair, Dassault Systemes** — CAE / CFD with robotics
  integration. Heavy mechanical-engineering culture; sim is one
  product line of many.

## Sim-native startups founded or scaled 2020-2025

This is where rapid growth (and compensation) is. Many of these did
not exist 4 years ago.

- **Applied Intuition** (founded 2017, valued **$15B Mar 2025** at
  $600M Series F) — AV simulation + ADAS / defense test platforms.
  Hiring aggressively across CA / MI / DC. Sim engineers cleared
  $300-450k TC.
- **Parallel Domain** (founded 2017, scaled 2023+) — synthetic data
  for AV / perception. Acquired by Applied Intuition in 2024.
- **Foretellix** (founded 2018, scaled 2023+) — scenario-based AV
  validation. Israeli / US offices.
- **NODAR, Cognata** (AV sim adjacent) — both raised meaningful
  rounds 2023-2024.
- **Genesis Project** (CMU + collaborators, 2024) — open universal
  sim. Pre-product / research-focused; will likely spin out a
  company.
- **Hillbot** (2024, ex-Stanford) — generative sim assets via
  diffusion models.
- **Spear AI, Cogniteam, Foxglove** — observability + sim-adjacent
  tooling for robotics teams. Foxglove (2021) is the most
  established; ex-Cruise founders.
- **Hello Robot, Reflex Robotics, RAI Institute** — robotics
  product companies with internal sim teams.
- **Cesium ion + Cesium for Omniverse** — geospatial digital twins;
  the dominant 3D-tiles platform.
- **Skild AI, Physical Intelligence, Figure, 1X, Apptronik,
  Generalist** — all have growing internal sim / synthetic-data
  teams to feed VLA training. Comp at the high end ($350-600k TC at
  senior IC).
- **Matterport, Polycam, Luma AI, Scaniverse** — consumer-side
  3D capture, increasingly used by industrial customers.
- **Diffuse Bio / Diffuse Drive** (small but emerging, 2024+) —
  diffusion-based sim asset generation. Watch list.
- **NVIDIA Cosmos partner ecosystem** — a growing number of new
  startups building products on top of Cosmos world models. Likely
  a meaningful 2026 hiring source.

## How they hire (so you can target effectively)

Most teams above hire sim engineers in three buckets. **The realistic
target for a junior web dev with ~6 months of ramp is "sim software
engineer" — bucket 2.**

- **Research scientist (sim / RL)** — PhD or equivalent; publishes
  at CoRL / RSS / ICRA / NeurIPS. Hardest to break in cold.
- **Sim software engineer** — strong eng + working ML + comfortable
  with USD / Isaac Sim / MuJoCo. You'll build envs, write training
  pipelines, package synthetic data, integrate scans. **The realistic
  target for a junior web dev.**
- **3D / USD / asset engineer** — overlaps with technical artist;
  Houdini, Blender, USD, lots of pipeline work. Comp slightly
  lower; entry path easier.

## Where sim engineers earn the most

Approximate TC bands for senior IC (3-7 years exp), 2025-2026 Bay
Area / NYC / Seattle:

- NVIDIA: $300-450k+ (median $270k for all levels; staff/principal
  $500k+)
- Applied Intuition: $300-450k
- Apple, Meta Reality Labs: $300-500k
- Google DeepMind: $300-500k
- TRI: $250-400k
- Foretellix, Parallel Domain (now Applied Intuition): $230-380k
- Top humanoid startups (Figure, 1X, PI, Skild, Apptronik):
  $350-600k+ (heavy equity)
- Industrial / CAE primes (Siemens, ABB, FANUC, Ansys, Dassault):
  $180-280k
- Sim-adjacent tooling startups (Foxglove, Spear AI, Cesium): $200-
  350k

US / EU remote roles are usually 20-40% lower; London is a partial
exception thanks to DeepMind + Wayve compression.

## Hiring market signal

From the 2025 Robotics Salary Guide and market reports:

- The Robotic Simulator market is **software-dominated** (~72%
  software share in 2025).
- Physical-AI simulation + digital-twin market projected to grow
  **$3.8B (2025) -> $34.6B (2034) — ~28.5% CAGR**, the highest of
  any segment in this analysis.
- NVIDIA has hired aggressively around Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab
  through 2024-2025. As of writing they had hundreds of open Isaac-
  adjacent roles.
- "RL" carries a **+33% salary premium** when listed as a required
  skill; "Isaac" / "Omniverse" / "USD" each show similar premiums
  in 2025 postings.

Translation: this is currently the most-aggressive growth segment in
robotics by both market CAGR and headcount. Less explosive comp than
the top humanoid VLA startups, but a much broader employer pool and
the easiest "pure software, zero hardware" path into robotics that
exists.
