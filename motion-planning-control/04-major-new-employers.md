  controls hiring.

- **Agility Robotics** — Digit, scaled commercial deployments 2023+.
- **ANYbotics** (ETH spinout, established) — ANYmal industrial
  inspection.
- **Apptronik** — Apollo controls team.
- **NVIDIA Isaac controls** — locomotion benchmarks team.
- **SpaceX, Joby, Archer** — aerospace controls.
- **Waymo / Zoox / Cruise** — vehicle controls.
- **Toyota Research Institute** — soft robotics + controls.
- **Honda Research Institute** — humanoid controls.

## Where controls engineers earn the most

Senior controls engineers at Boston Dynamics, SpaceX, Tesla Optimus,
Figure, and 1X hit $250-400k TC. The premium for "RL" on top of
classical controls is the +33% Reinforcement Learning skill premium
from Salary Guide 2025. Controls + RL is one of the most undersupplied
intersections in the market.
