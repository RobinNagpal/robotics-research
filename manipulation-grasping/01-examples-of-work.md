# Examples of Work

## Deployed systems

- **Covariant Brain** (acquired by Amazon 2024) — generalist bin-picking
  at large 3PLs.
- **Pickle Robot** — truck-trailer unloading with mixed-SKU picking.
- **Symbotic, Berkshire Grey, RightHand Robotics** — warehouse pick.
- **AutoStore + GreyOrange + Geek+** — case-picking.
- **Chef Robotics** — food assembly at production lines.
- **Dexterity AI** — multi-arm logistics (parcel, palletizing).
- **Soft Robotics Inc / Mujin / Realtime Robotics** — gripping + motion
  planning for picking.
- **Sanctuary AI Phoenix, Figure 02, 1X NEO, Apptronik Apollo** — dexterous
  humanoid manipulation demos.

## Landmark research

- **Dex-Net 1-4** (Berkeley, 2016-19) — generative grasp networks +
  large synthetic datasets.
- **GraspNet-1Billion** (2020) — billion-grasp dataset.
- **Contact-GraspNet** (NVIDIA, 2021) — fast 6-DoF grasp from point
  cloud.
- **TossingBot** (2020) — learned tossing for high-throughput sort.
- **DextrAH-G / DextrAH-Hand** (NVIDIA, 2024) — dexterous in-hand
  manipulation in sim-to-real.
- **In-hand cube reorientation** (OpenAI, 2019) — RL-based dexterous
  manipulation.
- **Diffusion Policy / 3D Diffusion Policy** — SOTA on many manipulation
  benchmarks.
- **Aloha + ACT / Mobile Aloha** — bimanual teleoperation + learning.
- **FoundationPose, NIDS-Net** — 6-DoF pose for grasp targets.

## Open-source pillars

GraspNet, Contact-GraspNet, AnyGrasp, ManiSkill 3, Robosuite, RoboCasa,
LeRobot, MimicGen, ALOHA codebase, Genesis (2024).
